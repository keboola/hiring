## One transformation / project you owned

Digital transformation of Ukraine's national statistics office (Ukrstat, 5000 staff), delivered by EPAM (2022-2026).

- **Problem:** 20+ fragmented legacy systems, manual statistical production, no agreed target state. Later, the war made field data collection dangerous.
- **What I owned:** the Digital Transformation Concept. I led the team that wrote it; the structure, ~60% of the text (ppl written, before GenAI was introduced) and the final edit are mine. It defined the target architecture, process model and roadmap and became the basis of the implementation program, which I then ran as client-side program manager (up to 70 people on vendor's side).
- **Scope:** unified data platform (Azure, SDMX), data explorer, online survey platform, automated scanner-data collection for CPI, StatGPT ("talk to your data", EPAM AI DIAL).
- **Measured result:** 2–3x faster end-to-end production cycle for the data products in scope; data collection kept running through the war; StatGPT live on stat.gov.ua.
- **Lesson:** the technology was ready before the organization was. I now measure live use, not go-live.
